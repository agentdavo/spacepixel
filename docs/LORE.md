# PROJECT VANGUARD — Series Bible

> *"Every gate is a throat. Every jump is a hymn."*
> — graffiti found aboard the derelict MCDF frigate *Lantern Guard 12*

## Premise

**2319. The Meridian Reach.**

Eighty years ago, survey ships found the **Lanterns**: vast rings of black
alloy orbiting dead and dying stars, each one silently holding a fold in
space open to another Lantern light-years away. Nobody knows who built them.
Surveyors called the builders the **Cartographers**, because the only thing
they seem to have left behind is the map.

Humanity poured through. Colonies bloomed in a dozen systems linked by
Lantern jumps, and the **Meridian Concord** was founded to hold the network
together — one government, one fleet, one timetable of jumps.

Then the Lanterns started *singing*.

It began as noise on the jump-comm bands: a low harmonic that appeared a few
seconds before every transit. Most people never heard it. Some people could
not stop hearing it. They called themselves the **Sable Choir**, and they
believe the Lanterns are not doors but *throats* — that the Cartographers are
still out there, speaking, and that humanity was never meant to walk through
the gates, only to answer them.

Six years ago, the Choir answered. At **Tessaly Gate** three Cathedral-class
dreadnoughts emerged from a Lantern that had never been charted, and the
Concord lost an entire fleet in forty minutes. Tessaly went dark. So did two
more systems since.

You fly for **Vanguard** — the MCDF 13th Independent Squadron — the unit
that goes through the gate *first*.

## Factions

### Meridian Concord Defense Force (MCDF)
*"Hold the Lanterns. Keep the light."*

The military arm of the Concord. Proud, overstretched, and increasingly
running on prototypes and old carriers pulled out of reserve.

- **Look:** ivory hulls, cobalt panels, signal-orange ID bands. Clean chamfered
  plating, visible panel lines, twin-engine layouts. Late-80s/early-90s
  mechanical design sensibility — every ship looks like it has a model kit.
- **Doctrine:** carrier groups project fighter wings through a gate ahead of
  the heavy ships. Fighters are variable-geometry so they can fold down into
  carrier cradles and survive atmospheric entry.
- **Key ships:**
  - **VF-27 Kestrel** — variable-geometry interceptor, the squadron's
    workhorse. Swing wings, canards, twin canted tails.
  - **SB-9 Warhorse** — heavy strike bomber built around a torpedo bay (M8).
  - **FFC Lantern Guard** — gate picket corvette (M8).
  - **CVS-07 Hesperus Dawn** — Vanguard's mothership, a battered fleet carrier
    with a hangar deck you can fly through (M9).
  - **BB Indomitable** — the last Concord dreadnought in the Reach (M9).

### The Sable Choir
*"Every gate is a throat. Every jump is a hymn."*

A transhuman movement of gate-listeners. Their ships are grown and cut
rather than welded: faceted violet-black hulls with crystal fins that glow
magenta when their resonance drives spin up. Choir pilots broadcast the
hymn on every open channel during combat. It is unsettling on purpose.

- **Look:** deep violet and black, knife-edged forward-swept blades,
  glowing magenta crystal "eyes" and seams, cathedral silhouettes.
- **Doctrine:** swarm interceptors screening colossal Cathedral dreadnoughts
  whose spires are resonance towers — they can *pull* a gate open.
- **Key ships:**
  - **SC-4 Cantor** — interceptor with forward-swept blades and a crystal eye.
  - **SC-7 Psalter** — torpedo bomber (M8).
  - **Vesper** — escort corvette with a spinal lance (M8).
  - **SCD-1 Cathedral** — 2.6 km dreadnought; spires, flying-buttress fins,
    a "rose window" main lance in the prow.

### Rustwake Salvage Compact
*"Nothing in the black is ever truly lost."*

Scavenger guilds living in the debris fields of the Rustwake Belt. Neutral,
mercenary, and the only people who know every unlisted jump route. Their
ships are rust-orange and olive, patched together from both sides' wrecks.
Their aces can be hired as wingmen.

### The Cartographers
The builders of the Lanterns. Never seen. Their only artefacts are the gates
and the **Lattice** — a faint geometric structure visible in hyperspace that
every pilot swears is different each time. In later chapters the Lanterns
begin to change shape...

## Tone

1990s OVA space opera: earnest pilots, operatic enemies, painted skies, beam
spam, missile circuses, dramatic freeze-frame cut-ins, and capital ships so
large they have weather. Hope, loss, and the question of whether some doors
should stay closed.
