# Combat and audio improvements

25 September 2026. Implementation following the [weapon, shield and audio review](COMBAT-AUDIO-REVIEW-2026-09-25.md), on the shared integration baseline `b0c8535`. This is the tested package for the existing 28 registered hulls. Future hull expansion must rerun these checks.

## Delivered in priority order

1. **Reliable contact and truthful feedback.** The broad-phase bound includes the offset shield ellipsoid and mounts. Every ship uses its fitted shield shell for contact; short steps no longer pass through the ends of capital shields. Catalogue stats now correctly give Resolute, Tallow, Longhaul and Umbra their four facings and capital subsystems. Small bare hulls retain the existing gameplay sphere. Missile contacts use the same shell/hull query as guns. Beam contacts on small bare hulls project their visual mark onto the skin.
2. **Correct event selection.** Intercepted missiles do not play player-damage cues. Shield, bleed-through and hull sounds follow the actual damage layers. Player impacts and shield state changes stay audible when the camera moves away. Loudness selection precedes beam/bleed cooldowns; up to three simultaneous missile detonations survive the event budget. Missile and beam hits provoke neutral ships consistently with bolts.
3. **Distinct weapons and beam lifetimes.** Fourteen guns, the capital lance and three missile families have authored identities. Light lasers, harmonic weapons, kinetic recoil, heavy drivers, flak, torpedoes and harpoons use different attacks, pitch, body and decay. Beam bodies follow the live emitter for the beam's lifetime, release when it stops, and are capped at eight. The total SFX pool remains 40 voices; diagnostics count dropped and stolen voices. Cinema audio snapshots retain weapon identity, actual damage, interceptions and beam lifetimes.
4. **Clearer mix and controls.** Separate external-combat and cockpit buses keep radio ducking from suppressing personal damage warnings. Reverb is shorter and lower. The Audio button provides saved Master, Music, Effects and Dialogue levels, Full/Reduced dynamic range, output choice, reset and speaker test. Capture mode (`shot=1`) hides the controls.
5. **Stereo, headphones and discrete 5.1.** Stereo remains the default. Headphones use HRTF positioning. Supported six-channel destinations use L/R/C/LFE/SL/SR routing, centered recorded/synthesized dialogue, low-pass bass contributions and a linked six-channel limiter that preserves direction. Unsupported destinations fall back to stereo and explain why. A six-channel WAV speaker-identification artifact is generated by the verification script. Optional operating-system speech remains outside Web Audio speaker routing; new speech utterances respect Master and Dialogue levels.
6. **Repeatable audition.** `?scene=combat&stage=audition` provides every registered hull and all 18 weapon profiles, full/weak/empty initial shields, firing distance, camera distance, cockpit/external gun sound and Restore target. Existing combat stages now feed audio, and frozen stages no longer consume the same visual/audio events repeatedly.

## Balance corrections

Correct classification exposed a shield-transfer mismatch on compact catalogue corvettes. They now transfer at three times the large-capital baseline: 0.009 of total capacity per second before fit multipliers, still well below the fighter baseline of 0.12. Base shield capacity, Resolute's 4,500 hull and its stock Mk II equipment remain unchanged.

Point defence previously tested a moving missile as a stationary point. A missile could cross a bolt between updates. Bolt interception now tests the relative motion of both objects for the tick. No extra fitted-PD burst-cadence tuning was imported.

The original balance bands remain unchanged. Stock Resolute wins the six-seed Lantern Guard duel average in **89.9 s with 27.2% hull left**; Mk III wins in **70.4 s with 57.3%**. In the fixed 36-missile swarm, Resolute intercepts **2/36**, Lantern Guard **14/36**; the swarms still threaten both ships.

## Practical limits

**Physical 5.1 remains unverified.** This machine's browser destination exposes two channels; its fallback works. Six-channel processing, speaker isolation and routing were verified with real Web Audio offline contexts, not a connected six-speaker listening test. Use Audio → 5.1 speakers → Test speakers on a compatible output to verify the physical mapping and levels. No calibrated listening-panel judgment is claimed.

The matrix checks damage/contact behavior, not every possible weapon/hull pair in a separately rendered flight. Native visual checks cover the three combinations above. Stations, asteroids and salvage wrecks remain outside weapon collision gameplay; this package does not add environmental destruction. Missiles retain designated-target guidance/contact rather than introducing interception by arbitrary intervening ships. Frozen trailer exports were not regenerated or represented as using this new mix.
